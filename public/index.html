What is a TASK certificate?

TASK — The Assessment of Sustainability Knowledge — is an international certificate that measures a person's understanding of environmental, social and economic sustainability. Employers and universities rely on it, which makes forged certificates a real problem.

Recognised credential

TASK scores are used in admissions and hiring around the world, so authenticity matters.

Fakes are rising

Edited screenshots and forged PDFs are increasingly common. Manual review is slow and error-prone.

Instant validation

Our validator cross-checks the certificate image and ID against the issuing registry in seconds.

How it works

1

App sends

The member captures the barcode in the mobile app, which sends the image in.

2

Analyse

The barcode's QR code is decoded automatically from the received image.

3

Cross-check

The decoded link is matched against the official TASK issuing register.

4

Review

Reviewers monitor the verdicts — authentic or not — in the console.
